## Brand & Style

This design system defines an energetic, impeccably trustworthy, and frictionless mobility and tourism transit service tailored to Nha Trang’s vibrant coastal environment. The aesthetic merges modern tropical leisure with the prompt, utilitarian precision of metropolitan ride-hailing.

### Core Character & Emotional Resonance
- **Trustworthy & Punctual:** Crisp layouts, distinct high-legibility typographic hierarchies, and explicit safety and pricing badges deliver immediate reassurance to international tourists and local commuters alike.
- **Vibrant Coastal Optimism:** Refreshing maritime blues and cyan waters meet high-energy transit ambers, capturing Nha Trang's sun-drenched coastal highway atmosphere without sacrificing commercial utility.
- **Effortless Clarity:** Form-first interfaces, immediate pricing transparencies, and uncluttered booking workflows prioritize rapid scanning under harsh outdoor sunlight.

### Design Movement: Modern Coastal Utilitarian
The system rejects heavy skeuomorphism and excessive blur in favor of a crisp, Modern Corporate-Hybrid approach: structured fluid grid mechanics, generous white breathing space, refined micro-borders, and targeted amber accents to drive key operational decisions.

## Colors

The palette balances maritime depth, clear coastal skies, and urgent transit cues:

- **Primary (`#0F2942` - Coastal Deep Blue):** Anchors foundational structural elements, top navigation bars, key headlines, deep contrast surfaces, and authoritative interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7` - Ocean Cyan):** Powers interactive auxiliary controls, route highlights, secondary tags, vehicle class selectors, and live GPS map tracking vectors.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Taxi Amber):** Reserved strictly for high-priority operational interactions—primary booking buttons, immediate action banners, ratings, departure countdowns, and urgent alerts.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate):** Regulates secondary body content, inactive icons, divider strokes, and subtle contextual metadata.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** Crisp, cool `#F8FAFC` ensuring optimal contrast against vibrant coastal imagery.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Sheets):** Pure `#FFFFFF` with precise 1px borders in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Inputs, Well Areas):** `#F1F5F9` for tactile interactive targets.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and interactive labels with **Be Vietnam Pro** for long-form contextual details, booking summaries, and multi-language Vietnamese/English readability.

- **Headlines & Display:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides clean geometric modernism with open apertures that remain crisp in bright ambient lighting conditions.
- **Body & Captions:** Be Vietnam Pro ensures native diacritic rendering for Vietnamese street addresses, tour itineraries, and passenger legal notices without visual crowding.
- **Labels & Badges:** Semi-bold to bold weights emphasize vehicle classes (e.g., 4-Seater Sedan, 7-Seater SUV, Island Tour Van), pricing figures, and flight terminal identifiers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible 12-column system on desktop and tablet, collapsing into a 4-column stack on mobile. 

### Breakpoints & Layout Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Booking forms switch to full-width stacked fields with anchored bottom-screen CTA bars for single-handed usage.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Search and vehicle selection display in dual-pane modular cards.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, max container width of 1280px, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 2rem`. Split-screen hero layout pairs the primary booking engine with an interactive Nha Trang route preview.

### Spacing Philosophy
Consistent vertical rhythm utilizes multiples of 8px (`0.5rem`). Elements within card modules rely on `space-sm` and `space-md`, while sections and major transit options are isolated by `space-lg` to `space-xl` to prevent input fatigue during travel planning.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through crisp, light-bathed surface layers rather than heavy dark shadows, maintaining a fresh coastal aesthetic:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Background `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, dedicated to structural canvas and ambient travel maps.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Modules):** Pure white background with a subtle border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and a soft ambient drop shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Booking Drawers & Popovers):** Elevated overlays utilizing an oceanic-tinted shadow: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.10), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Live Ride Trackers):** Deep anchor elevation: `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.16)`, accompanied by a high-contrast `#0F2942` backdrop wash at 40% opacity.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a unified **Rounded** posture (`roundedness: 2` = `0.5rem` / `8px` default radius). 

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Minor Controls:** Built with standard `0.5rem` (`8px`) corners to communicate dependable, modern software architecture.
- **Booking Containers, Modals, and Tour Cards (`rounded-lg`):** Styled at `1rem` (`16px`) corner radius to create inviting, approachable travel packages.
- **Badges, Route Chips, and Floating Indicators:** Employ fully pill-shaped borders (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical tags, ratings, and vehicle capacities from clickable rectangular buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Now / Confirm Ride):** Energetic Amber (`#F59E0B`) background with bold deep navy text (`#0F2942`). Hover state darkens to `#D97706`. Height is 48px on mobile and 52px on desktop to provide large thumb-friendly touch targets.
- **Secondary Action (View Route / Tour Details):** Transparent fill with a 1.5px border in Ocean Cyan (`#0284C7`), text colored `#0284C7`. Hover transitions to `#0284C7` with white text.
- **Ghost/Tertiary Action:** No border, text in Coastal Deep Blue (`#0F2942`) with standard underline on hover.

### Inputs & Booking Form Fields
- **Pickup / Drop-off Fields:** Height 52px, background `#FFFFFF`, 1.5px border `#CBD5E1`. Left-aligned with contextual location pins (Ocean Cyan for pickup, Energetic Amber for destination).
- **Focused State:** 2px stroke in `#0284C7` with a 3px soft outer ring in `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Vehicle Selectors
- **Vehicle Type Selectors (Sedan, SUV, Limousine):** Card-style interactive chips featuring vehicle silhouette, capacity badge, and estimated price. Inactive: `#FFFFFF` border `#E2E8F0`. Active: `#FFFFFF` border 2px `#0284C7` with an ambient cyan glow.
- **Amenity / Tag Chips:** Pill-shaped, small (`label-sm`), background `#F1F5F9`, text `#0F2942`. Active or selected chips shift to `#0284C7` text with `#E0F2FE` background.

### Trust Signals & Badges
- **Driver Rating & Quality Badges:** Pill-shaped with `#FEF3C7` background, text `#92400E`, accompanied by an amber star icon.
- **Guaranteed Pricing Badge:** 100% Fixed Price icon tag with `#DCFCE7` background, text `#166534`.
- **Tour Operator Verification:** Verified shield icon rendered in `#0284C7` next to operator headlines.

### Cards & Tour Listings
- **Transit & Tour Cards:** Composed of an edge-to-edge scenic image (Vinpearl cable car, Cam Ranh coast, Po Nagar towers), anchored on white backing with 16px padding. Price callouts are right-aligned using `headline-md` in Deep Blue (`#0F2942`).
